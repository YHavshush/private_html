About Us

An Independent Firm Committed to Your Success

Founded by former bulge-bracket bankers, Meridian Capital Partners was built on a simple conviction: clients deserve dedicated senior attention — not associate hand-offs — on every engagement from mandate through close.

We bring deep sector expertise, a global network of strategic buyers, and unwavering independence to every transaction. Our principals have collectively advised on over $4 billion in completed deals across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

What We Do

Advisory Services

We provide a focused range of investment banking services to middle-market companies, family-owned businesses, and private equity sponsors.

01

Mergers & Acquisitions

Buy-side and sell-side advisory for strategic acquisitions, divestitures, management buyouts, and carve-outs. We run highly disciplined processes designed to maximize value and certainty of close.

  • Sell-side representation
  • Buy-side advisory
  • Management buyouts
  • Divestitures & carve-outs
02

Capital Raising

Equity and debt capital solutions tailored to your stage and strategy. From growth equity to leveraged finance, we connect clients with the right capital partners for long-term success.

  • Growth & minority equity
  • Leveraged finance
  • Mezzanine & subordinated debt
  • Recapitalizations
03

Strategic Advisory

Independent financial guidance for boards, management teams, and special committees navigating complex decisions. We provide the analysis and perspective to act with confidence.

  • Fairness opinions
  • Board advisory
  • Strategic alternatives
  • Shareholder matters
04

Restructuring

Comprehensive restructuring advisory for companies navigating financial distress, creditor negotiations, or operational transformation. We protect value when it matters most.

  • Out-of-court restructuring
  • Creditor negotiations
